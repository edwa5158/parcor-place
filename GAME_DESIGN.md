# Parcor Place — Game Design

Designed by an 8-year-old game designer, written down by Claude.

## Story
A dragon has had a spell cast on it and **can't fly**. To break the spell it must
run and jump through **100 parkour levels** and collect **100 magic gems**.
When all 100 gems are collected, the spell breaks and **the dragon can fly again!**

### Cutscenes
The story is told in cartoon cutscenes with voices and music:
- **The start:** the dragon loves to fly, but it zooms past the grumpy **Wizard Grumbleboots**
  and knocks his pancakes right off the plate. Splat! He casts a spell ("Abra... ca... GRUMPY!"),
  and hides 100 magic gems all over the land.
- **The ending:** the gems glow, the spell pops, and the dragon flies again. The wizard says sorry
  (he was just hungry), they make pancakes together, and fly off as best friends.
- **Getting to a new world:** a quick scene in that world. Wizard Grumbleboots pops in (poof!),
  more and more worried as the gems add up (10, 20, 30...), and hungrier and hungrier: his tummy
  rumbles in Lava Land, and by his tower he's asking if it's lunchtime yet. That's why he was so grumpy.
  It plays the first time you start a world; the **📖** button on the map plays it again.
- **Losing all 3 hearts:** a short funny or cheering line from the wizard, the narrator, or the dragon.

Tap to go to the next picture, or **Skip**. The **📖 Story** button on the title screen plays the start again.

## How you play
- Side-view platformer (run and jump).
- iPad touch controls: on-screen buttons **⬅️ ➡️** and **JUMP**.
- Each level has **one magic gem**. Grab it to finish the level.
- **No bad guys.** Only obstacles: gaps, lava, spikes, and tricky jumps.

## Obstacles
Every world has its own things to jump over and around, so the levels don't all feel the same.

**Things to get over:** boxes (a chimney on the rooftops, a tree stump in the treetops, a present
in the clouds, a stack of spell books in the wizard's tower...), wide **bushes** and **tumbleweeds**
you can hop over or run across, pointy **roofs** and **hills**, and gentle **sand dunes** and puffy cloud bumps.

**Ouchies,** and each one takes up a different space:
- **Spikes:** short and wide. They look different in each world: thorns, spears, lightning bolts,
  black lava glass, ice spikes, spinning saws, magic spikes.
- **Crystals:** taller than spikes, so jump a little sooner.
- **Cactuses:** thin but tall. Big ones are two tiles tall and need your biggest jump.
- **Icicles, stalactites, hanging crystals, ceiling spikes, dripping goo:** they hang from a ceiling.
  Run under them and **don't jump!** (A vault over a box is low enough to be safe.)

The first time a world shows something new, a hint says what to do.

## Parkour moves
All with the same three buttons:
- **Vault:** run fast into a crate and the dragon hops right over it.
- **Ledge grab:** jump at a wall that's a little too tall and the dragon grabs the edge and climbs up.
- **Wall slide:** hold toward a wall while falling to slide down it slowly.
- **Wall jump:** press JUMP on a wall to kick off it. Hold toward the wall and keep tapping JUMP to climb up a really tall wall.
- **Slide:** run into a low tunnel and the dragon slides through on its belly.
- **Roll:** land from a big drop while running and the dragon does a parkour roll.

The first world teaches one new move every couple of levels, with a hint the first time.

## Hearts
- The dragon has **3 hearts**.
- Touching lava or spikes, or falling off, costs 1 heart and the dragon goes back to a safe spot.
- Lose all 3 hearts and the level starts over.

## Make your own dragon
Players create their dragon and give it a name. Choices:
- **Dragon type:** European (four legs, big wings), Eastern (long and snake-like), and more
- **Body color:** red, blue, green, purple, gold, and others
- **Horns:** different styles (or none)
- **Wing style and wing color:** bat wings, feathery wings, and others
- **Eye color**

## Worlds (10 levels each, 100 total)
Requested: rooftops, trees, castles, clouds, lava, spikes.

## The ending
After the 100th gem the spell breaks, the dragon flies, and there's a celebration.

## Technical notes (for the grown-up)
- Planned as a web game (HTML5 canvas) that runs in iPad Safari and can be added to the Home Screen like an app.
- Progress (gems, unlocked levels, the dragon) is saved automatically on the iPad.
